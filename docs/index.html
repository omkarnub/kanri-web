Kanri Documentation Hub

Welcome to the technical and operational documentation for Kanri (v1.19.8, Build 75). Kanri is an offline-first personal finance ledger engineered for Android 8.0+.

Architectural Foundation

Kanri runs 100% on-device with zero developer-operated databases or tracking servers. All transaction processing, regex parsing, budget calculations, and visualizations happen inside the local Android process.

Documentation Chapters

1. Getting Started →

Download APK, verify SHA-256 hash, grant Android permissions, and configure your first budget cycle.

2. Capture & Dedupe →

Dual SMS receiver + notification listener, 5-minute sliding window deduplication, and OTP filtering.

3. Cash & Online Wallets →

Dual ledger architecture, ATM cash withdrawal transfers, and net worth balance synchronization.

4. Lend, Borrow & Split →

Tracking receivables, one-tap WhatsApp payment reminders, UPI deep-link settlement, and bill splitting.

5. Budgets & Goals →

Custom cycle start days (1–28), real-time Safe-to-Spend daily allowance, savings pacing, and quick steppers.

6. Insights & Health Score →

18+ visualizations, GitHub-style spending heatmaps, burn-rate projections, and the 4-pillar 0–100 health score.

7. 12 Pixel DotTiles Widgets →

Nothing-inspired dot-matrix widgets, Ndot57 font, platform Canvas renderer, and battery-safe midnight reset.

8. Themes & Backdrops →

5 visual themes, Liquid Glass design, 22 animated backdrops, AGSL GPU half-precision, and 4 launcher icons.

9. Backup & Restore →

PBKDF2 encrypted local backups (.enc), restricted Google Drive sync (drive.file scope), and CSV/JSON/PDF exports.

10. Security Architecture →

SQLCipher AES-256 at rest, Android Keystore hardware protection, BiometricPrompt, and threat model.

11. Troubleshooting & OEMs →

Fixing aggressive OEM battery killers (MIUI, ColorOS, HyperOS), notification listener watchdog rebinds, and permissions.
