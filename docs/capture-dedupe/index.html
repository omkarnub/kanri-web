2. SMS & Notification Capture & Deduplication

An in-depth explanation of how Kanri ingests bank SMS alerts and UPI notification popups, executes 5-minute sliding-window deduplication, and discards OTP messages.

1. The Ingestion Pipeline

In modern Indian banking and UPI ecosystems, a single digital transaction generates two distinct signals on your Android phone:

  • Bank SMS (Authoritative Accounting): Contains official debit/credit confirmation, account last-4 digits, official balance, and UTR reference number.
  • Payment App Notification (Rich Metadata): Emitted by Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or CRED; contains merchant business name (e.g., "Swiggy", "Blue Tokai Coffee") and recipient VPA.

Kanri captures both signals concurrently in local device memory, fusing them into a single enriched record with zero data duplication.

2. Priority 999 SMS Receiver

The SMS receiver is registered via SmsReceiver.kt with priority 999: 

<receiver android:name=".receiver.SmsReceiver"
    android:exported="true"
    android:permission="android.permission.BROADCAST_SMS">
    <intent-filter android:priority="999">
        <action android:name="android.provider.Telephony.SMS_RECEIVED" />
    </intent-filter>
</receiver>

When an SMS arrives:

  1. Pre-Filter: Kanri checks sender headers against BankSmsPatterns.kt prefixes (e.g. SBIPAY, HDFCBK, ICICIB, AXISBK). Non-banking personal chats are immediately dropped.
  2. OTP Guard: If the text contains OTP indicators (OTP, one-time password, secret code, valid for 10 mins), parsing stops immediately. The message is dropped from memory and never persisted.
  3. Regex Parsing: Extracted amount, account ending, transaction mode (UPI, Card, ATM, NetBanking), and UTR are normalized.

3. Notification Listener Scoping

KanriNotificationListenerService.kt runs strictly in the background and is scoped exclusively to authorized financial package names:

  • com.google.android.apps.nbu.paisa.user (Google Pay)
  • com.phonepe.app (PhonePe)
  • net.one97.paytm (Paytm)
  • com.dreamplug.androidapp (CRED)
  • com.fampay.in (FamPay)
  • in.org.npci.upiapp (BHIM)

Notifications from messaging apps (WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal) or social platforms are completely ignored at the Android binder boundary.

4. The 5-Minute Sliding-Window Deduplication Algorithm

Because SMS delivery times vary based on cellular network latency, an SMS may arrive 10 seconds before or 4 minutes after a UPI notification.

NotificationDeduplicationHelper.kt enforces a bi-directional 5-minute sliding window:

  1. When a transaction arrives from either source, Kanri queries the local Room database for matching candidates where |t_incoming - t_existing| < 300,000 ms (5 minutes).
  2. UTR Match: If the 12-digit UPI reference / UTR matches, records are fused immediately.
  3. Amount & Account Match: If the UTR is missing or delayed, Kanri matches identical amounts and account last-4 digits within the same time window.
  4. Metadata Enrichment: The generic SMS counterparty (e.g. UPI/432618920192/PAYMENT) is automatically updated with the human-readable merchant name from the notification (e.g. Swiggy).

5. 1-Tap Floating Category Overlay

When SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW permission is granted, Kanri renders a subtle, translucent floating chip on your screen for 5 seconds immediately following an in-store payment. Tapping the chip lets you tag the category (e.g. "Dining", "Groceries", "Transport") without opening the full application.
